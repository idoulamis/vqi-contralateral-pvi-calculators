How the calculators were developed

Each model is an ensemble of 50 bootstrap-aggregated, shallow CART regression trees trained separately to estimate cumulative contralateral-PVI risk at 12, 36, and 60 months. Death before the later PVI was treated as a competing event; inverse-probability-of-censoring weights were used and truncated at 20. Patients were split once into training and test sets (80/20), with stratification by observed PVI, death, or censoring.

The no-contralateral-WIfI model was fit in 70,410 patients; the model with contralateral WIfI was fit in 40,945. On the internal test sets, AUC ranged from 0.583 to 0.594 without contralateral WIfI and from 0.587 to 0.605 with it. These results indicate modest discrimination. Neither model has been externally or temporally validated.

The calculator runs in your browser. Values entered into the form are used locally by the page and are not sent to a server. The published page contains model rules but no patient-level records.
