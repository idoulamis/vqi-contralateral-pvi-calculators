Contralateral PVI Risk Calculator With Contralateral WIfI

Estimated probability of a first subsequent contralateral PVI at 1, 3, and 5 years after index PVI.

Who this applies to: adults in the VQI PVI CLTI cohort used for development: unilateral index PVI, infrainguinal-only target, no recorded prior revascularization or major amputation, survival of the index admission, and no ambiguous same-month contralateral ordering. This version was fit in 40,945 patients with contralateral WIfI stage 1-4 and usable follow-up time; 2,135 otherwise-eligible patients with a recorded contralateral stage lacked usable event/censor time. This model is a research prototype; it has not been externally validated and is not a clinical treatment recommendation. Inputs stay in this browser and are not transmitted.

Held-out test performance

CART tree ensemble trained on 32,645 patients and evaluated on a separate patient-level 20% holdout (N=8,300). Split was stratified by observed PVI, death, or censoring. AUC and Brier score use inverse-probability-of-censoring weights (IPCW); weights were truncated at 20. Higher AUC and lower Brier score indicate better discrimination and prediction accuracy, respectively. Discrimination is modest in this internal test set.
